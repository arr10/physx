## Not found / skipped

The following 3 exercises did not have a suitable, clearly-licensed demonstration
image on Wikimedia Commons after a thorough search (full-text search, category
browsing, and Wikipedia infobox lookups). No image was substituted from another
source, per instructions:

- **Wall sit** — no dedicated demonstration photo/diagram exists on Commons; the
  linked image on the "Imaginary chair" Wikipedia article (`Airline_position.svg`)
  does not actually depict the exercise.
- **Russian twist** — no image found under this name or related search terms
  (oblique twist, seated torso rotation, etc.) in the File namespace.
- **Mountain climber (exercise)** — searches only surfaced rock/mountain climbing
  photography; no bodyweight "mountain climber" exercise image was found.
